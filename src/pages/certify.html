Six steps

  1. ApplyYour revenue band and your AI providers set the tier.
  2. Connect usageA billing export, a gateway key, or the signed collector.
  3. Receive your footprintTokens become liters under the Standard, derivation attached.
  4. CommitThe fee, plus ten times your footprint at the water price or the tier minimum, whichever is greater.
  5. We fund and proveRegranted to the best-evidence programs, confirmed from outside, retired once.
  6. Display the badgeYour registry entry goes live with a verification ID. One year.

Audited

Over $100M

$20,000minimum a year

$5,000 plus your own auditor. Water: 10x your footprint, or $15,000.

The water price is set each year from the actual cost of the prior year's grants, $1 per 1,000 liters as the version 1 placeholder. Every tier's water floor is three times its fee, so at least 75 cents of every dollar is water. Exact figures lock after the pilot cohort. A 100x tier is available.

Worked example

A company spending about $100,000 a year on a frontier API has a footprint near 150,000 liters. It pays $1,500 to be checked and $4,500 for water: 4.5 million liters, about 2,250 person-years of safe water, about 6% of its AI spend.
