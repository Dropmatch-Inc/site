How to read an entry

Verified usageTokens pulled from the company's providers and reconciled to its accounts payable, under the tier shown.
FootprintThat usage converted with the Standard's factors, scope 1 plus scope 2.
Water fundedThe volume delivered through the named project, with the person-years the evidence supports.
ProjectLinks to the grant, its evidence (chlorine tests, GPS, photos, sensors) and the other funders sharing credit.
StatusVerified, Pending or Revoked. A revoked entry stays listed with the date and the reason.
