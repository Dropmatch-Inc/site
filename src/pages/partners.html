How a program qualifies

Evidence firstRandomized trials or outside evaluations of health or access outcomes, with the cost per person-year published.
Proof of deliveryChlorine tests, GPS and photos, flow sensors. Delivery counts only when confirmed from outside.
Restricted money, named fundingThe program accepts restricted grants routed through Dropmatch and allows the funding to be named on the public ledger.
Additional and counted onceWater delivered because of the grant, with credit split among funders in proportion to what they paid.

Year one funds programs in developing countries only. Same-basin replenishment near data centers is a later track, labeled separately.

How water is counted

Volumes follow Volumetric Water Benefit Accounting 2.0 and the WASH Benefits Accounting Framework: 20 liters per person per day, the lower of the capacity-based and beneficiary-based estimate, actual users rather than the whole village, uptake discounts, and the words "contributed to". Every unit is retired once in the registry.
